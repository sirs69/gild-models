How it works

      
There are no menus. You walk. Tap the ground to go there, tap a building to go to its door — when you arrive, that building's business appears below the city. Buy materials at the market, carry them to your workshop, let it work, carry the goods back and sell them. Hire hands at the tavern, buy standing at the church, buy rank at the town hall. Time runs whether or not you do anything.

      
      
An original game in the spirit of the medieval town sims — Europa 1400 and The Guild 2. Not affiliated with those games or their makers.
